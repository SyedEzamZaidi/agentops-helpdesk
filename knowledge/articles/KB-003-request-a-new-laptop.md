# KB-003: Request a new laptop

**Applies to:** Replacement and new-joiner laptops · **Audience:** All employees and line managers · **Last reviewed:** 04 Oct 2026

## When you can request one
- Your laptop is more than **4 years old**, or
- It is faulty and IT has confirmed it cannot be repaired, or
- You are a manager requesting equipment for a new joiner.

A slow or freezing laptop is usually fixable: raise a ticket first rather than requesting a replacement.

## Steps
1. Open the **IT Service Portal** and select **Hardware → Laptop request**.
2. Choose the request type: **Replacement**, **New joiner** or **Faulty device**.
3. Select the standard model for your role (Standard, Developer or Executive). Non-standard models need a business justification.
4. Enter your cost centre and submit.
5. Your line manager approves the request. Replacements above the standard model also need IT approval.
6. IT contacts you to arrange collection and data transfer.

## Good to know
- Back up anything stored only on the old laptop to **OneDrive** before handover.
- Return the old laptop to IT within two weeks of receiving the new one.
