# KB-006: Connect your phone to the company Wi-Fi

**Applies to:** iOS and Android · **Audience:** All employees · **Last reviewed:** 04 Oct 2026

## Which network
- **Rabudzu-Corp:** company laptops and enrolled phones. Gives access to internal systems.
- **Rabudzu-Guest:** personal devices and visitors. Internet only.

## Connect a company-enrolled phone to Rabudzu-Corp
1. Open **Settings → Wi-Fi** and select **Rabudzu-Corp**.
2. Sign in with your company email address and password (EAP method: PEAP, if asked).
3. Accept the certificate **radius.rabudzu.com** when prompted.

## Connect a personal phone to Rabudzu-Guest
1. Select **Rabudzu-Guest**.
2. A sign-in page opens. Enter your company email address to receive a daily access code.

## If it does not work
- **Certificate warning shows a different name:** do not connect. Report it to the helpdesk agent as a possible security issue.
- **Keeps asking for the password:** forget the network and connect again; check your password has not expired.
