# KB-007: Printer troubleshooting

**Applies to:** Office multifunction printers · **Audience:** All employees · **Last reviewed:** 04 Oct 2026

## Quick checks
1. Check the printer's display for an error (paper jam, low toner, offline).
2. Make sure you selected the right printer. Floor printers are named by location, for example **PRN-F3-01**.
3. Cancel stuck jobs: **Settings → Printers & scanners →** your printer **→ Open print queue → Cancel all**.

## Common problems
- **Blank pages:** usually an empty or unseated toner cartridge. Check the toner level on the display. Do not open the printer beyond the toner door; report it if a new cartridge does not fix it.
- **Faded or streaky prints:** toner is low. Facilities replaces toner; report it through the helpdesk agent.
- **Printer offline:** another user may see the same. If everyone on the floor is affected, report it as an outage.
- **Paper jam:** follow the diagram on the display. Do not pull paper out from the rear unit.

## If it does not work
Raise a ticket with the helpdesk agent and include the printer name and the error shown on the display.
