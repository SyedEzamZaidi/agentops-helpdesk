# KB-008: Suspected phishing or compromised account

**Applies to:** All accounts and devices · **Audience:** All employees · **Last reviewed:** 04 Oct 2026

## If you clicked a suspicious link or entered your password
Act immediately:
1. **Change your password** at **mysignins.microsoft.com → Password**. Use a new password you have not used before.
2. **Contact the IT security team** straight away through the helpdesk agent (say "I clicked a suspicious link") or call the security line. Do not wait to see if anything happens.
3. Do not delete the email. Security may need it.

## Signs your account may be compromised
- MFA prompts you did not trigger. **Never approve a prompt you did not start.**
- Emails in your Sent folder that you did not send.
- New inbox rules forwarding or deleting mail.
- Password changed without your knowledge.

## Reporting a phishing email you did not click
- In Outlook, select the message and use **Report → Report phishing**.

## What happens next
IT security reviews your sign-ins, revokes active sessions if needed, and contacts you. Account recovery for someone else's account is never done on request through chat; it always goes through IT security.
