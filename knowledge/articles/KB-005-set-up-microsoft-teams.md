# KB-005: Set up Microsoft Teams on your laptop

**Applies to:** Company laptops (Windows) · **Audience:** All employees · **Last reviewed:** 04 Oct 2026

## Steps
1. Teams is pre-installed on company laptops. Open **Microsoft Teams** from the Start menu.
2. If it is missing, open **Company Portal**, search for **Microsoft Teams** and click **Install**.
3. Sign in with your company email address and approve the MFA prompt.
4. Go to **Settings → Devices** and select your headset, microphone and camera. Use **Make a test call** to check them.
5. Go to **Settings → Notifications** and set how you want to be alerted.

## If it does not work
- **Signed in with the wrong account:** select your profile picture → **Sign out**, then sign in with your company account.
- **No audio in meetings:** check the device selected under Settings → Devices and that the headset is not muted.
- **Teams will not start:** quit it from the system tray, then open it again. If that fails, restart the laptop.
- Still not working: raise a ticket with the helpdesk agent.
